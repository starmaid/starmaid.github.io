## What even is nexus knockout?

Basically check out the [Robot Combat Events website](https://www.robotcombatevents.com/events/6477) for a rundown. Its at Maker's Nexus in Sunnyvale, CA. Its full combat up to 3lbs, plus bot hockey. This was the same event, two years prior, that I learned about the sport and got inspired to try it myself ([See that blog post here](/blog/_posts/2025-07-20-hockey-robot.md)). This time, I had my own bot.

### Did you do anything to prep since open sauce 2025?

One massive change: I ordered laser-cut aluminum panels for my robot. Exact same design, but more able to take a beating than the acrylic.

![alternate acrylic panels](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/panels.jpg)

*The alternate battery covers, with the clean robot behind them. I didn't want to lose the clear battery cover...*

I reprinted a lot of the robot for fresher, stronger parts than immediately after open sauce. I also got rid of the last internal PLA parts and replaced them with TPU.

![picture of it at night near my car](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/night_practice.jpg)

*Night Practice near Scott's house*

## The Games

This time, rallying under the unified team Scottbot, we had a full 6 bots - Scott with 1 and 2, Orion with his 3, and me with my single competitor. Lucy was also there to get some experience driving. We were also lowkey cracked. 

![team photo](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/team_photo.jpg)

![dr pepper](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/dr_pepper.jpg)

### Morning

Group stage. We lost to No-Ice (just by two points - very close game) and crushed the hockeymaniacs.

![group stage bracket](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/nexusknockout3_groups.svg)

We knew No-Ice was going to be tough, and our team was still getting our footing. Hockeymaniacs had some good bots, but I think they were having some hardware issues.

![working on the bots](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/bree_and_orion.jpg)

### Lunch Break

My friend Bree showed up to hang out, and brought her RC drift cars. They got a bit torn up on the parking lot, but were a lot of fun.

## The rest

Smashed The Party Animals, but theyre always fun to play. Met team No-Ice in the finals, and had one of the lowest-scoring matches I think I've ever seen. Defense was extra good here.

![elimination bracket](/blog/img/2026-04-04-nexus-knockout-3/nexusknockout3_bracket.svg)

My bot managed to stay mostly cool, but at the end of the last match the motors were hotter than I had ever seen them. Definitely was driving hard, in direct sunlight.
